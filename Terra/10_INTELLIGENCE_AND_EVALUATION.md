## Metrics and comparisons

- Retrieval: Recall@k = relevant retrieved / relevant total, Precision@k, Hit@k, MRR, nDCG@k if graded labels available; explicit denominator and aggregation, handle unanswerables separately; measure destination ranking separately from chunk ranking.
- Generation: citation validity (IDs), claim support/entailment (human or configured judge rubric), factual answer accuracy, abstention accuracy on unanswerables, hallucinated POI count; citation validity does not imply support. Judge metrics not available without judge or reviewed labels; label model and rubric/version if used, separate human/deterministic/judge provenance. No fixed faithfulness values.
- Planning: exact day count, overlaps, selected POI validity, evidence presence, budget math, complete vs incomplete cost coverage, known budget compliance, route feasibility/unknown rates. Replace heuristic preference score with measured preference evidence coverage, not hardcoded 0.9.
- Operational: p50/p95 warm/cold retrieval and total latency, actual provider usage, estimated USD API costs with price version, failures, index build time and corpus size. Local retrieval external API charge zero; local compute cost not calculated. Do not call estimated model charge actual invoice.
